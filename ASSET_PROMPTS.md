# 이미지 제작 메모

제작 방식: 내장 이미지 생성 도구. 장면 이미지 및 확대 이미지 아틀라스 생성 후, 웹앱에 필요한 각 타일을 분리하고 WebP로 저장했습니다.

공통 프롬프트: scientific-educational. Korean grade 4 immersive ecosystem exploration web app. Wide realistic natural history illustration with photographic textures, natural lighting, clearly recognizable living organisms in native habitat. No text, labels, panels, borders, fantasy.

- 바다: Korean coastal sea cutaway, seagull upper left, sunshine upper right, alive octopus lower left, living brown kelp lower middle, living coral colony lower right, fish middle right, clear water, rocks and sand. Above-water rocky shoreline. Accurate anatomy.
- 강: Korean freshwater river cross section, mallard duck left, grey heron right bank, reeds and cattails right foreground, freshwater fish left underwater, green filamentous algae right underwater, water strider at middle surface, stones, soil bank, sunlight.
- 숲: Korean temperate oak woodland clearing, living oak tree right, red fox left middle, red squirrel lower right trunk, ants lower left close enough to identify, living purple asters lower middle, mushrooms near tree base, soil and stones, sunbeams.
- 확대 카드: photographic contact sheet, exactly six columns and three rows, equal tiles, no gutters or labels. Row 1 seagull / octopus / brown kelp / coral / marine fish / mallard; row 2 grey heron / cattail / freshwater fish / filamentous algae / water strider / oak; row 3 red fox / red squirrel / ant / purple aster / mushrooms / ferns. Clear living subjects in natural habitat with realistic anatomy.
